Why any of this is here

Online, a visitor cannot see, hear or touch the person behind a page, which leaves a credibility gap. A named person, a real location, a direct email and a stated response time are the cheapest ways to close it — so they go above the form, not buried under it.

Frequently asked

Do I need a Google account to submit the form?

No. The form accepts responses from anyone with the link, and sign-in is not required. If you hit a permission screen, that is a bug — email me and I will fix the setting.

What happens to my response?

Responses land in a private spreadsheet used only to improve this site. Nothing is shared, sold, or added to a mailing list, and the email field is optional.

Can I see the source code?

Yes. The whole site is four HTML files, one stylesheet and one script, versioned in a public Git repository and deployed from it. I built it by hand for my web development coursework.

The form will not load on my network.

Some campus and corporate networks block embedded frames. Use the direct link under the form, which opens the same form in a new tab.
