How I approach it

Whether it is a marketing campaign, a hiring push or a web page, the planning questions barely change.

  1. Step 1Who is the audience?

    Not demographics for their own sake, but what they want and why they are here now.

  2. Step 2What is the offer?

    Stated plainly and early. If it takes a paragraph to explain, it is not ready.

  3. Step 3What should they do next?

    One clear next step per page or post. More options means fewer decisions.

  4. Step 4Test it on someone else.

    Give them a task and watch. A failed task is not arguable.

  5. Step 5Change one thing at a time.

    Otherwise you learn that something improved without learning what.
